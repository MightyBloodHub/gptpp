# Scalar authoring pilot, 19 November 2026

GPT++ accepted **0 of 24** submissions. Typed Python accepted **3 of 24** and Rust accepted **0 of 24**. Across identical task assignments, GPT++ used **44.5% more total tokens than Python** and **15.3% more than Rust**. GPT++ had zero accepted work, so its tokens and seconds per accepted task are undefined. This run supplies no evidence for the proposed 20% savings.

GPT++ reached a terminal lexer or parser error on 18 of 24 trials. The other six returned incorrect public results. Python and Rust each had three additional submissions that passed public checks and then failed on held-out cases. All three accepted Python submissions succeeded on the first call.

The low acceptance rate limits the comparison. Only two task families produced any accepted work. DeepSeek Coder reached its 240-token output limit on 40 of 69 calls across the three languages. Llama 3 reached the cap on 0 of 68 calls. The benchmark covers 12 scalar task families, two quantized local models, and one seed per condition. The zero-width Rust acceptance interval reflects their identical failures in this sample. The run describes the current full-function workflow under this budget.

The final run restarted after correcting the mypy adapter so repairs could receive the actual type diagnostic. The data contains the complete corrected run. The toolchain, model digests, test coverage and prompt traces are recorded with the results. [Protocol and reproduction instructions](../README.md) · [Raw trial log](2026-11-19.jsonl)

## Detailed results

The planned pilot has 72 trials (12 tasks × 2 models × 1 seed × 3 languages). The log contains 72 completed trials. The observed or declared roster has 12 tasks, 2 models and 1 seed values.

Coverage uses the identities declared in the run manifest.

All 72 cells on this roster are present.

Completed trials include 36 synthesis and 36 maintenance assignments. Totals include failed calls and repairs. Model, tool and trial wall times are reported separately.

Comparisons pair the same model, seed and task. Each estimate uses its available pairs. Brackets give 95% percentile intervals from 10,000 task-cluster resamples with seed 20261119. Each sampled task retains all its matched model and seed runs. Undefined ratio draws may occupy either tail. One task cannot estimate sampling uncertainty.

The design's 20% target requires a cost-ratio upper endpoint of 0.800 or lower. Its acceptance margin requires a lower endpoint of at least −2 percentage points. Tokens and elapsed time measure different aspects of computational effort. Qualification requires a separate study and a defined cost model.
